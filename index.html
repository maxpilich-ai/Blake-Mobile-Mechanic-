What I do

Services I bring to your driveway

Most jobs done in one visit. Anything I can't handle on-site, I'll tell you straight up — no wasted trips, no surprise bills.

Suspension Repair

Struts, shocks, control arms, bushings, ball joints. Riding rough? I'll diagnose and fix on-site.

Brake Repair

Pads, rotors, calipers, fluid flushes. Squeaks, grinding, soft pedal — I diagnose the real issue.

Charging System Repair

Battery, alternator, starter. Won't start? Dimming lights? I'll test on-site and have you running.

Diagnostics

OBD-II scan, code read, and a real explanation of what's wrong — never a guess-and-replace game.

Fleet Services

Keeping a fleet running? I'll come to the lot, work through it, and bill the company directly.

Trailer & Small Engine

Trailer lights, bearings, hitch wiring. Mowers, snowblowers, ATVs — if it has an engine I can work on it.

About Blake

I started this because I was tired of seeing folks get jerked around by shops.

I'm Blake Conavatti. I started Blake Mobile Mechanic in 2024 to give folks in Otsego and the surrounding towns a straight-shooting option for auto repair.

Every quote is upfront. Every diagnosis comes with a real explanation. If a job is beyond what I can do mobile, I'll tell you and point you to someone honest. That's the deal.

I work on cars, trucks, vans, trailers, and most light-duty equipment. I'm not a chain. One guy, one stocked van, who shows up when he says he will.
